## E1: Personalized audit offer
Hypothesis: a verified, specific page problem earns relevant replies from owner-led painters.
Audience: first 50 screened US painting businesses; initial research focus Wisconsin. Two message variants, 25 each, assigned in alternating order after eligibility screening. No company receives both. Metrics: delivered, hard bounces, opt-outs, meaningful replies, qualified calls, paid deposits. One follow-up after five business days; stop on any response.
Initial maximum: five new contacts/day, ten total emails/day including follow-ups. Stop for any complaint, broken opt-out, or authentication failure. Pause list review at two hard bounces in 50. These small-sample rules are conservative operating choices, not statistical proof.
After 50 delivered and 14 days after last follow-up, revise if zero relevant replies. Continue only if at least three relevant replies or one qualified call, without safety failures. A small difference between variants is directional evidence only. Do not declare a winner from one reply.
Budget: existing sender costs only; no purchased list or enrichment subscription. Sending remains disabled until exact contacts, messages, address, and limits are approved.

Negatives: jobs, job, salary, course, tutorial, templates, template, free website builder, diy, painting classes, paint colors, house painter near me. Use phrase negatives where multiword intent matters. Review actual terms; do not block “free audit”.
Sitelinks after deployment check: audit, services, about, design demos. Final URL must be the approved landing page. No unsupported price, turnaround, ratings, or revenue claims.
Bid proposal: Manual CPC if available; otherwise Maximize Clicks with $3 CPC cap. This cap is an economic assumption. Keyword Planner volume and auction bids are unknown. If capped traffic is unavailable, do not increase automatically.
