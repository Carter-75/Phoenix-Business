Frequently Asked Questions

HOW WE WORK &
COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our AI-accelerated development process, human engineering oversight, and service guarantees.

What is Phoenix Websites AI?

Phoenix Websites AI is an AI-native web development company that builds custom websites and full-stack web applications for clients. AI accelerates the development process, while senior human engineers provide architecture, review, security audits, and delivery.

Is Phoenix Websites AI an AI website builder?

No. We are not a DIY website builder, a template marketplace, or a prompt-it-yourself tool. Clients hire us to handle every phase of design, custom engineering, testing, and managed hosting.

Do customers have to write prompts or build the website?

No. Customers never write prompts or wrestle with code. You simply provide your business requirements, goals, and content; our engineering team executes the AI workflows and human quality control.

How is AI used in development?

AI is used to accelerate the development lifecycle: generating component scaffolding, creating automated unit tests, drafting responsive CSS, and writing boilerplate logic. This cuts turnaround time by 50% or more.

Are humans involved and what do they oversee?

Yes, 100%. Carter Moyer and senior engineers oversee system architecture, database schema design, security boundaries, API authentication, code review, responsiveness, and final quality assurance.

Can Phoenix Websites AI build custom full-stack applications?

Yes. We build custom full-stack web applications, authenticated client portals, admin dashboards, relational/document databases, Stripe payment workflows, and third-party API integrations.

Who owns the finished website and code?

You own 100% of your finished website code, design, and intellectual property. There is zero proprietary agency lock-in; you can host with us or self-host at any time.

How quickly can projects be completed and how much does it cost?

Starter custom websites start at $1,499 and deliver within 2 weeks. Comprehensive business websites start at $2,499 and deliver within 3 weeks. You can configure your exact requirements on our interactive pricing configurator.
