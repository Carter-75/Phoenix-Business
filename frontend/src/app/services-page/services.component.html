Frequently Asked Questions

How does Phoenix Websites AI differ from a DIY website builder?

With DIY platforms (like Squarespace, Wix, or WordPress templates), you are expected to spend dozens of hours figuring out design, mobile responsiveness, plugins, and hosting. When you hire Phoenix Websites AI, you hire Carter to plan, engineer, launch, and maintain a custom-built website tailored specifically to your goals.

How does AI assist in the development process?

We use AI-assisted developer workflows to generate component scaffolding, run automated testing passes, and explore layout variations rapidly. This eliminates boilerplate delays and speeds up delivery while Carter directly engineers and tests the final architecture, security, and aesthetics.

What are the delivery timelines?

Every tier has a maximum delivery deadline from onboarding completion: Simple Launch delivers in 2 weeks maximum; Essential Care delivers in 3 weeks maximum; Professional Growth delivers in 4 weeks maximum. Enterprise Custom projects follow a custom milestone schedule agreed upon before work starts.

How does the Lifetime Price Lock Guarantee work?

Your monthly subscription rate for your website contract is locked for life. As long as your plan remains active, your monthly fee for that website will never increase, protecting your business budget against future price inflation.
